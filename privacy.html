Privacy policy

3D.whatif · last updated 11 November 2026

Who we are

3D.whatif is an independent creator channel publishing computer-generated simulation videos. "We" means the person who runs the channel.

This website

This website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

The 3D.whatif publishing app

The 3D.whatif publishing app is a personal tool used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos to the channel's own TikTok, YouTube and Facebook accounts.

  • What it accesses: only the channel's own accounts, after the owner signs in and grants permission. Permissions used: basic account information (to identify the account), uploading videos, and publishing posts (title, caption, privacy setting).
  • Where data is stored: the access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local configuration file that is never published. Nothing is stored on any server we run.
  • What it does not do: it does not read, collect, sell or share data about viewers or any other user; it does not read messages, followers, contacts or analytics; it does not post on anyone else's behalf.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time in the security / connected-apps settings of each platform (TikTok, Google Account, Facebook), which makes the stored tokens useless.

Viewers of our videos

When you watch or comment on our videos, you do so on TikTok, YouTube or Facebook, under those platforms' own privacy policies. We only see what those platforms show to any channel owner (such as public comments and aggregate view counts).

Children

Our content and tools are not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. For any privacy question, contact us through the 3D.whatif channel on TikTok, YouTube or Facebook.
